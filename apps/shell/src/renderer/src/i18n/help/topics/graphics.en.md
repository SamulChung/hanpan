# AI Photo and AI Illustration

Two tabs for pictures. **AI Photo** opens photos and layered pictures (PSD, PSB, PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF, HEIC). **AI Illustration** opens vector art (SVG, SVGZ, AI, VectorCraft). Each tab runs the free PhotoCraft or VectorCraft app inside GenOffice, with an AI assistant beside it.

## Opening and creating

- On Home, press **AI Photo** or **AI Illustration** for a new picture. Open a file from the open dialog, recent files or the folder tree, or drag it onto the window. One tab holds one picture.
- Installing GenOffice does not make it the default program for picture files.

## Saving

- **Save** (Ctrl+S) writes the picture back to its file. **Save As** (Ctrl+Shift+S) asks for a place and a format.
- A photo with layers, type or masks is saved as PSD so nothing is lost. A note tells you when that happens.
- Closing a tab with unsaved changes asks first, as in the other editors.

## Quick actions

- The buttons above the app do common jobs without AI. AI Photo: **Remove background**, **Auto tone** and **Export at size**. AI Illustration: **Trace image**, **Center on artboard** and **Export PNG or SVG**.
- Export asks for the format and the size, then where to save the copy. The picture itself does not change.

## AI assistant

- Tell the AI what to change, for example "Remove the background" or "Draw a blue rounded rectangle in the middle". It works through the app's own commands, and you see each change.
- While the AI works you cannot edit the picture. Press **Stop** to end the request.
- After a request that changed the picture, **Undo** under the reply takes back everything that request changed. If you edited the picture after it, use the app's own undo (Ctrl+Z) instead.
- The AI cannot save, open or close files. It gives you a PNG, JPEG or SVG copy through the save dialog.

## What is sent

- The AI receives your request, a short summary of the picture (its name, size, layers and selection) and, for a model that can see images, a small picture of the canvas. The two apps send nothing themselves. More in [The AI assistant panel](help://ai-panel).
