# Getting started with Hanpan

Hanpan sends what you sorted out in a mind map to a Hangul (HWP) report, a slide deck or a Word report in the same window, and keeps the map and the document in step. The AI on this PC comes first.

## Send a mind map to a document

1. In the mind map toolbar, press **Send to a document** and pick a HWP report, PowerPoint or a Word report. For PowerPoint, **Slide style** offers ten designs, from Steve Jobs and McKinsey to a startup pitch deck.
2. Choose the layout (point-form report, report with chapters, official letter numbering), the date and department, and whether to send **exactly what the map says** or **let the AI write the sentences**.
3. Press **Send**: the document opens in a new tab of the same window. Its first save asks where to keep it.

## Sync the map and the document

- Press **Send to a document › Sync with linked documents** to see what changed in the map and in the HWP document.
- Changes made in the map go to the document, and changes made in the document come to the map. Where both sides changed a topic differently, choose the map's, the document's, or later.
- Sentences written in the document are kept. Word and PowerPoint documents can be sent but not synced back yet.

## Hanpan templates

- The **Hanpan templates** card on Home opens 19 templates (official letters, reports, proposals, minutes, plans) as HWP or Word.
- In the **Markdown converter** card on Home, paste Markdown or open a .md file and press **Convert all**: every format you ticked (HWP, HWPX, Word, ODT, PDF, HTML) is made side by side in one folder.

## Hanpan tools

- **Hanpan tools** in the strip above a HWP tab tidy and check an official letter, change its tone for a generation, mark a three-colour review and build a structured prompt.
- Tools that change the document show the result in the tab right away. Closing without saving leaves the file as it was.

## Design

- The **Design** card on Home makes posters, card news, notices and thumbnails.
- Press **Copy to paste in a document**, then Ctrl+V in a HWP, Word or PowerPoint tab to put the page in as a picture. Pages also save as PNG and PDF.
- **Send to a document › Send to card news** in a mind map makes one card per main branch.
- Press **✦ AI helper** at the top right of the design window to open the helper on the right. Under **Make new**, describe what you want and send it: the AI you chose in Settings writes the words and Hanpan lays them out as an editable design. **Fill this design** keeps the current layout and colours and rewrites only the text boxes with your content; after picking a template, **✦ Fill this template with my content** does the same. With a text box selected, a ✦ bar above it polishes, shortens, makes it catchier or translates between English and Korean in one click. Every result has **Undo**, and if no AI is connected yet you can connect one right in the helper.
- In the design window, the **Templates**, **Elements** and **Text** tabs add templates, shapes, QR codes and text styles. Shift-click or drag across empty space to pick several, move them along the guides, resize and turn them by their handles, and double-click a text box to type in place. The right side sets gradients, shadows, outlines and photo filters, the **Layers** tab sets order and locks, and **Resize** changes the page size keeping the layout. Pages also save as JPG.
- Pick several and press **Ctrl+G** to group them so they pick and move together; **Ctrl+Shift+G** ungroups. Right-click an element or empty space for copy, paste, order and lock. Double-click a photo and drag to choose what shows, and zoom it under **Crop** on the right. **Draw** on the **Elements** tab draws handwriting or highlighter strokes, the **Rulers** button at the top measures positions, and dragging with Space held moves the view. Fonts added with **Add a font file…** in the font list are saved inside the design file.
- The **Templates** tab has 20 samples, among them a notice card, a school letter, an event poster, a recruiting notice, a menu board, a certificate and a slide cover. They use the fonts that come with Hanpan (Pretendard, Noto Serif KR, Gowun Batang, Black Han Sans, Do Hyeon and more), so they look the same on any PC. Under **Icons** on the **Elements** tab, search for a word such as "calendar" or "place" to add a line icon, then change its colour and line width on the right.

## The AI on this PC

- At first start Hanpan looks for an AI running on this PC (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM) and, if you have not chosen an AI, uses it.
- To use another AI, choose it in **Settings › AI**.
